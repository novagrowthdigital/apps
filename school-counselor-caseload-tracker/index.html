The problem: a counselor's week is hard to see in one place

Sessions get noted on sticky notes or in a calendar, referrals arrive by email and hallway conversation, parent calls need a follow-up date, and 504 and IEP meetings come around on their own schedule. When it is time to show how your time was spent, the numbers have to be pieced together.

This workbook gives each of those its own simple log. You type each thing once, and Home and Use of Time add it up for you.

How a typical week works

Once, at the start of the year

On Start Here, type your name, pick your role and the month your school year starts. Paste your roster into Caseload by initials or student ID, with grade, referral reason, case status and plan. Add students with a 504 plan or IEP to Meetings with their annual review and re-evaluation dates.

After each session

On Session Log, each session is one row: date, type and minutes. Add a student for one-to-one work, or leave Student empty for a group or a classroom lesson, which is logged once and still counts toward your use of time. Each session type counts as direct, indirect or program and support, from a list you can edit.

When a referral comes in

On Referrals, log the date, student, source, reason, action taken and status. Days open count on their own, and the oldest open referrals show first on Home.

After a parent call or email

On Parent Contact, log the method, topic and a follow-up date, and mark it Done when it is finished. Overdue follow-ups show on Home, soonest first.

When you need your use-of-time numbers

Use of Time totals your minutes by month in three buckets (direct, indirect, and program and support) and shows your student services share against your own target, with a chart. The target starts at 80 percent; change it on Start Here to match your school's plan.

Who it is for

School counselors at any grade level who keep a caseload, log sessions and track referrals, parent contacts and 504 and IEP meeting dates. School social workers and school psychologists who log the same kinds of work can use the same tabs; pick your role on Start Here.

What it does not do

  • It is a spreadsheet, not a FERPA- or HIPAA-certified system. Store it the way your district's policy requires.
  • It does not connect to your student information system.
  • It is not clinical, legal or compliance advice. The use-of-time target is a planning aid.
  • It has no name, birth date or address fields. Use initials or IDs; every note column reminds you not to type full names.

Questions before you buy

Is student data private?

The data stays in your own file; we never see it. Use initials or IDs, not full names.

How many students and sessions fit?

300 students, 2,000 sessions, 500 referrals and 1,000 parent contacts. Insert rows above the last line of a tab for more.

How do I log groups and classroom lessons?

Log the group or lesson once and leave Student empty. It still counts toward your use of time.

Can I change the session types and the target?

Yes. Session types, referral reasons and contact topics are your own dropdown lists on Start Here, and so is the services target.

Is there sample data?

Yes. One copy has made-up practice entries and one is blank.

Is it compliance advice?

No. It organizes your data. Check meeting timelines against your district's own rules.
