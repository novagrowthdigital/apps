How to play

  1. Split into two teams. The host reads each question out loud.
  2. One player from each team answers first. The higher answer on the board wins control.
  3. The team in control guesses one answer per player. Click a tile (or press 1-8) to reveal a match; its points go into Round points.
  4. Wrong guess? Press Strike. Three strikes and the other team gets one guess to steal.
  5. Give the round points to the winning team, press Show all to reveal what nobody guessed, then Next.
  6. Most points after the last round wins. Bonus rounds pay double and triple.
